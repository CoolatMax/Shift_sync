The Problem & Solution
📱
Problem
A mess of WhatsApp messages, sticky notes & phone calls

Small business owners struggle to manage shift swaps. This leads to no-shows, frustrated staff, and owners spending hours redoing schedules every time someone falls sick or has an emergency.

📋
Solution
A lightweight, mobile-first shift management app built for small teams

ShiftSync gives owners a weekly schedule board, lets staff request and approve swaps with one tap, and sends automatic SMS reminders — no app download required for employees.

How It Works
STEP 01
🗓️
Build the weekly schedule

Owner drags and drops shifts on a visual board. Roles, timings, and staff are all in one place.

STEP 02
🔄
Staff request swaps

Staff tap "request swap" via SMS link. ShiftSync shows available colleagues who can cover.

STEP 03
✅
Owner approves in one tap

Owner gets a push notification and approves or denies swaps instantly. No calls needed.

STEP 04
📲
Auto-reminders sent

Everyone gets an SMS reminder 2 hours before their shift. No-shows drop dramatically.

Core Features
🗓️
Visual Schedule Board

Drag-and-drop weekly view with colour-coded roles. Export to WhatsApp or PDF with one click.

🔄
Smart Swap Matching

When someone can't make it, ShiftSync suggests available colleagues based on role, hours, and preference.

📲
SMS-first (No App Needed)

Staff interact via SMS or WhatsApp links. No app download barrier for part-time or older staff.

📊
Attendance Analytics

Track no-show rates, overtime alerts, and monthly hours per person. Simple enough for a small cafe owner.
